Summits – Track & Share Your Mountain Peak Adventures

Summits is the ultimate companion for mountaineers, hikers, and outdoor enthusiasts who want to track their summit achievements, discover new peaks, and connect with a global community of climbers. With over 450,000 mountain peaks from around the world, Summits helps you document your climbing journey and explore new adventures.

KEY FEATURES

Automatic Peak Tracking
Connect your Strava or Garmin Connect account and let Summits automatically analyze your routes to identify which peaks you've climbed. No manual entry required – simply sync your activities and watch as your summit collection grows.

Explore 450,000+ Peaks Worldwide
Discover mountains and peaks from every corner of the globe. Browse by country, region, elevation, or difficulty. Use our interactive map to explore new climbing destinations and plan your next adventure.

Track Your Progress
Monitor your climbing statistics and achievements. See how many peaks you've conquered, track your elevation gains, view your climbing history, and watch your progress over time.

Global Leaderboards
Compete with climbers from around the world! See how you rank globally, by country, or by region. Climb your way to the top of the leaderboards and showcase your mountaineering achievements.

Save Your Favorite Peaks
Build your personal collection of saved peaks. Mark peaks you want to climb, save your favorites for quick access, and create your own wishlist of future adventures.

Interactive Maps
Explore the world's mountains with detailed, interactive maps. Switch between satellite view, topographic maps, and terrain views. Tap on any peak to see detailed information including elevation, location, and nearby routes.

Community Features
Connect with fellow climbers, share your achievements, and discover new routes. Follow other mountaineers, see their summit collections, and get inspired by their adventures.

Seamless Integration
Summits seamlessly integrates with your favorite fitness platforms:
• Strava – Import your hiking, trail running, and mountaineering activities
• Garmin Connect – Sync data from your Garmin devices

All your activities are automatically analyzed to identify which peaks you've climbed based on GPS coordinates and elevation data.
